Privacy Policy

Reaktor collects no personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. Effective July 24, 2026.

No ads No analytics No accounts No data leaves your device
01

Who we are

Reaktor is a local multiplayer strategy game for Android, developed and published by Shravan Goswami. This policy covers the Reaktor mobile app distributed on Google Play and this website.

02

Data we collect

None. The app has no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no account system. It makes no network calls of its own and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

03

Data stored on your device

Game sessions, board settings, sound and haptic preferences, and the local leaderboard (including any player names you type during setup) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They never leave it. You can erase everything at any time from Settings (Clear All Game History, Clear Leaderboard) or by uninstalling the app.

04

Permissions

Reaktor requests no sensitive permissions: no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access. Links in the app (such as developer pages or the Play Store listing) open in your browser or the Play app, where those services' own policies apply.

05

Children

Reaktor is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

06

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process platform-level data (such as install statistics or crash telemetry) as described in Google's privacy policy. That processing happens outside the app.

07

This website

The site is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log standard request data as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Search runs entirely in your browser via a static Pagefind index. Your theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage.

08

Comments and feedback

Blog comments and the support page's feedback box use Giscus, which stores comments as GitHub Discussions in a public repository. Commenting is optional and requires signing in with your GitHub account; anything you post is public and governed by GitHub's privacy statement and terms.

09

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Reaktor ever adds features that touch user data (such as cloud sync), this page will be updated first and the effective date revised.

10

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@shravangoswami.com or open an issue on GitHub.
